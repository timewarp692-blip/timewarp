HOW TO PLAY

RUNYou circle the clock automatically. Tap the left or right side (or swipe, ←/→, A/D) to switch lanes: left is inner, right is outer.
DODGEHands and barriers block some lanes. Stand in a free lane as they pass.
CLEAREvery hurdle passed gives of game time.
GRABGlowing orbs: 🛡️ shield, 🐌 slow time, ⏩ time warp (+3 hurdles). Every 100 hurdles restores a life. Collect 💠 shards to buy skins.
RIDEA glowing gold hand sometimes sweeps up behind you. Be in its lane to hop on: it carries you at top speed, you can't be hit, and every hurdle you pass counts!
CHRONODodge to fill the ⏳ ring, then tap it (or swipe up / Space) for slow time and double points. Purple gates flip lanes: watch the dashed warning!
SURVIVEReach 24:00:00 to complete the day.
